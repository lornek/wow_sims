# Forever spellbook UI and roster

The spellbook follows the supplied WoW Forever Talents screenshot: attached school tabs (pale active tab), a parchment panel, a large school heading/count, a diamond divider, three columns of four spell rows per page, and round navigation arrows at the lower right. Icons, dark serif spell names, muted rank/level labels, and gold change markers sit on the parchment. Search and rank/level controls share the dark tab strip; status filters are below the frame. Two columns are used below 1050px and one below 600px. Tabs stay against the parchment when controls wrap.

`forever-spellbook.mjs` defines an explicit per-class roster of the existing Forever trainer spells and a reviewed list of active talent abilities from the project's Forever trees. The spellbook no longer consumes the entire shared catalog: records for passive talents, procs, removed spells, and unreviewed additions cannot appear. Missing active talent abilities are described from their Forever talent node. Resurrections remain available for browsing, even though they are not combat actions in Healbot. Trainer passives such as Reincarnation remain valid spellbook entries; passive *talents* such as Bloom, Illumination, Divine Aegis and Improved Regrowth do not.

No SoD item/gear database is used to construct spellbook data. A spell shared with SoD is allowed only if it is in the Forever roster. Adding an entry to the general catalog, including a SoD-only spell, cannot automatically expose it in the spellbook. New or changed Forever trainer spells and talent abilities require explicit roster review against the Forever client/reference data. Removed spells are excluded again at the pagination/filter layer, so searching or showing all ranks cannot reveal them. There is no Gone filter.

Default browsing shows the highest learned rank per spell, ordered by when the spell is first learned rather than its latest rank. All ranks shows the available ranks together. School, search, rank and status changes reset pagination; page numbers are clamped after filtering. Searches span schools. Active talent spells are labelled Talent ability. Classic-comparison markers use known source metadata; absent comparisons stay Other rather than being called unchanged.

School controls use tablist/tab/tabpanel semantics, selected states and a single tab stop. Left/Right/Home/End navigate schools. Hover, keyboard focus or tap opens the shared game tooltip. Healing tooltips retain final healing, scaling and talent calculations. Page/filter changes destroy previous tooltip handles; class unmount cleans up remaining handles. Navigation has accessible previous/next labels and a page-status announcement.

This change affects spellbook presentation and eligibility, not combat formulas or underlying spell values. Validation covers all four class rosters, passive/removed/foreign-spell rejection, rank ordering, pagination and DOM interaction checks. The supplied screenshot is the visual reference; browser/preview availability limits visual verification.
